## Editorial image matching

Route photographs depict a named route location, endpoint or relevant region. Captions identify the actual photographed place. A regional landscape used beside a public-transport route is not presented as a photograph of the train or coach journey itself. The photographs are historical views and should not be used to infer present conditions.
