## Background colour and texture · 背景配色的理由

Why these two: Dunedin's railway station pairs dark Kokonga basalt with pale Oamaru limestone facings; the site takes the same pair. Basalt is the dark ground (the hero, the map's sea, the footer), sandblasted so it reads as stone and not as a flat black screen; Oamaru limestone is the page, a warm, faintly pink cream with a honed, sandy grain, so it feels like a quarried block rather than paper or skin. Pounamu green stays for the brand and "reached" states; glacier-milk blue for water and highlights. Replaces the v3 slate/paper block (still in v3.css, overridden here).
